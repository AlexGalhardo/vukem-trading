Research carried out on 2026-11-03 (UTC).
Markdown content file; originally saved with the `.nd` extension and renamed to `.md`, as requested.
Scope: technical facts and dependencies for the personal Brazil/US terminal already
planned. The research does not activate integrations, does not execute orders and does not turn
still-open proposals into accepted decisions.

## 1. Method and evidence states

Primary sources were searched and opened: vendor documentation, official repositories, PostgreSQL and official B3/CVM pages. Search results
served to locate documents; blogs and aggregators do not support the
conclusions. Documentation pages were consulted; no datasets were acquired.

In this report:

- **Verified:** the consulted source declares the capability or restriction.
- **Implication:** engineering interpretation for vukem-trading, not a guarantee from the vendor.
- **Pending:** requires a decision, a test with the chosen account or contractual confirmation.

Prices, limits, terms and versions may change. Revalidate before contracting or
implementing; no authenticated broker access was used in this research.

## 2. Preserved product basis

Confirmed by the user: personal use; Brazil and US; backtesting and paper trading;
Alpaca Paper in the US; B3 internal simulator; single authorized Google account;
Go/Vukem, Inertia/React, Tailwind/PostgreSQL; dashboard at `/`, shadcn/ui sidebar,
visitor redirect to `/login`, Google only; Railway deploy.

Q7–Q9 remain pending: assets/directions, IEX feed + B3 CSV import and five-minute candles/regular session. This research informs these choices, without
answering on the user's behalf. See [grilling](/docs/project/grilling).

## 3. Alpaca Paper: capabilities and limits

### 3.1 Simulation environment

**Verified:** Alpaca offers a paper environment with its own credentials and endpoint. The documentation states that paper-only accounts are created globally; this does not prove eligibility for a real account. The simulation does not fully represent market impact, queue position, slippage from latency, regulatory fees
or dividends. Its liquidity assumption allows fills that may be unfeasible
in the real market. Source: [Alpaca Paper Trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading).

**Implication:** keep the paper account, ledger and metrics separate from backtest and
internal simulation. Display results as simulation. Validate the paper host at
boot and prevent accidental switching to live. Do not use paper to demonstrate that
the engine already models all costs, nor as proof of future return.

### 3.2 IEX, consolidated market and limits

**Verified:** the Basic plan table consulted presents real-time stock coverage via IEX, 30 symbols per WebSocket and 200 historical queries per
minute. The Algo Trader Plus plan advertises coverage of all US exchanges,
plus capacity and a price of US$ 99/month on the consultation date. The page also
reports a historical restriction on the most recent 15 minutes on the basic plan;
confirm its application to the feed and endpoint contracted before designing ingestion.
Source: [About Market Data API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api).

**Implication:** configure the feed explicitly. IEX volume and candles must not
be presented as consolidated volume of all exchanges. History and stream
need the same provenance or differences must be identified in the experiment.
Market data limits are not automatically order sending limits.

**Pending:** Q8 has not yet confirmed IEX. Check account permissions, historical depth,
effective limits and budget; do not subscribe to a plan automatically.

### 3.3 Order identification and tracking

**Verified:** the API allows assigning a `client_order_id` and retrieving the order by
this identifier. Source: [Working with orders](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/working-with-orders).
The `trade_updates` stream communicates fills, partials, cancellations and rejections;
the documentation distinguishes binary frames of the paper stream from data frames.
Source: [Websocket Streaming](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/websocket-streaming).

**Implication:** persist the intent and the ID before sending. On timeout, query
the state and reconcile, without blindly generating a new order. Deduplicate fills before
changing cash/position. Implement reconnection with recovery via API.

**Limit of the conclusion:** a queryable ID does not prove exactly-once execution
nor a distributed transaction with PostgreSQL. Test uniqueness, resubmission,
query latency and out-of-order events in the adapter. An isolated negative response
right after a timeout does not prove the order never existed.

## 4. B3: API, data and usage rights are distinct problems

### 4.1 Direct API access

**Verified:** the B3 for Developers portal states B2B service and no direct access to its
APIs for individuals.
Source: [B3 for Developers](https://developers.b3.com.br/).

**Implication:** the personal terminal must not assume a direct connection to the exchange
to send orders. The already accepted choice of an internal B3 simulator avoids this
dependency in the MVP. A future integration must be proven with the
broker: authentication, orders, cancellation, fills, positions and contract.
This does not mean every broker offers a public API.

### 4.2 Public history does not prove adequate intraday history

**Verified:** the official historical series page directs to files
organized by annual, monthly and daily periods.
Sources: [B3 historical series](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/market-data-e-indices/servicos-de-dados/market-data/historico/mercado-a-vista/series-historicas/)
and [file page](https://bvmf.bmfbovespa.com.br/pt-br/cotacoes-historicas/FormSeriesHistoricasArq.asp).

**Implication:** the existence of these files does not prove five-minute candles.
Before importing, verify the layout, granularity and adjustments of the
chosen product. Do not interpolate daily OHLC to fabricate an intraday path.
If the MVP requires five minutes, history with that resolution or finer will be needed,
with demonstrated origin and usage rights.

### 4.3 Commercial policy changed close to the research date

**Verified:** the official page distinguishes the policy in force until 31/10/2026 from
the policy from 01/11/2026, and separates commercial documents for 2026/2027.
The linked commercial PDF for late 2026 identifies validity from November
to December and application categories, including display and non-display.
Sources: [B3 policies and contracts](https://www.b3.com.br/pt_br/market-data-e-indices/servicos-de-dados/market-data/distribuidores/politica-comercial-e-contratos/)
and [commercial policy published for 2026](https://www.b3.com.br/data/files/85/35/A3/7F/5D881A10F75B881AAC094EA8/Politica%20Comercial%20de%20Market%20Data%20B3%202026%20V1.2.1.pdf).

**Implication:** do not reuse the old table as a budget. Confirm with the
vendor whether the contract covers historical storage, replay, signal calculation and
display on the dashboard. Personal use does not automatically demonstrate exemption from all
data rules. Do not conclude classification/licensing merely from the technical possibility of obtaining prices.

**Limitation:** the full Consumption Policy PDF linked on the page could not be opened by the web reader because it exceeded the size limit. No full review
of this document was performed, nor calculation of the applicable fee for vukem-trading.

### 4.4 brapi as a data candidate, without a vendor choice

**Verified:** the provider's own FAQ lists intraday intervals, including
`5m`, but describes intraday history of up to seven days. A separate table
presents depth per plan for history in general; it does not prove that a plan
with years of history provides years of five-minute data.
Source: [brapi timeframes](https://brapi.dev/faq/quais-sao-os-timeframes-disponiveis).

**Implication:** it is a candidate for investigation, not a contracted integration. Before
selecting it, test the required assets and intervals and confirm delay,
depth, origin, adjustment, availability, rate limit and permission for algorithmic
use. Quote documentation does not establish an order execution API.
A short window may serve to test the pipeline, but it is not enough to assess
strategy robustness across different market regimes.

**Pending:** Q8 and budget. CSV remains a proposal, not available data. Each
import will need a manifest of source/license, timezone, currency, timeframe,
interval, hash and adjustment rule; validation cannot presume a trusted origin.

## 5. Google authentication and Vukem support

### 5.1 Stable identity and verification

**Verified:** Google documents OpenID Connect and ID token validation; `sub`
is the stable identifier, while email may change. `email_verified` is a
distinct claim. The OAuth client, consent and callback must be configured.
Source: [Google OpenID Connect](https://developers.google.com/identity/openid-connect/openid-connect).

**Implication:** the confirmed bootstrap uses only the authorized verified email,
and then binds to the Google subject. Validate signature, issuer, audience,
expiration, state and nonce before creating a session. An email provided by the browser is not
authorization. Proposed minimum scopes: `openid email profile`. Recovery of
the binding must not reopen public sign-up.

### 5.2 Framework pinned in the project

**Verified in the documentation for tag v0.37.0:** `social.Google` uses OIDC; the flow
includes PKCE/state and ID token validation. The state storage must be
shared in production. The scaffold by default returns to the navigation intent or
`/dashboard` and has an account-linking policy based on verified email.
Source: [Vukem social login — tag v0.37.0](https://github.com/AlexGalhardo/vukem/blob/v0.37.0/site/content/docs/security/social-login.mdx).

**Implication:** adapt the destination to `/`, register Google exclusively,
apply the single-account admission before the session and review the generated
password/sign-up flows. Use persistent cache for states, with migrations. Do not accept
the scaffold's automatic linking without review as the owner's policy.

**Access limitation:** the Vukem site URL failed in this round in the web reader;
the confirmation came from the official document at the tag, obtained through the GitHub
connection. Do not presume current examples on `main` are identical to the installed version.

## 6. shadcn/ui and charts

**Verified:** the Vite guide provides for Tailwind and alias configuration in TypeScript
and Vite; it allows reusing the existing configuration. The sidebar requires a provider
and offers structural and control components.
Sources: [shadcn/ui Vite](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/installation/vite)
and [Sidebar Base UI](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/base/sidebar).

**Implication:** in vukem-trading, adapt paths to `resources/js` and
`resources/css/app.css`, without creating another parallel app. Choose one family of
primitives and keep consistency. The sidebar is navigation, not an authorization guard;
protect routes and endpoints in Go. Charts and tables must show source, currency,
simulation mode and the age of the last data.

**Verified:** Lightweight Charts is available under Apache 2.0 and its
repository requires an attribution notice and a link to TradingView. The
`attributionLogo` option satisfies the link part, but does not eliminate the rest of the obligation.
Source: [Lightweight Charts official repository](https://github.com/tradingview/lightweight-charts).

**Implication:** if chosen, pin the version, preserve licenses/NOTICE and implement
attribution. The charting library does not replace contracting market data.
It remains a candidate; it was not installed by this research.

## 7. PostgreSQL: precision and concurrency

**Verified:** PostgreSQL distinguishes exact decimals `numeric`/`decimal` from
approximate float types. `numeric` also admits special values such as `NaN`; the
possibility of infinity depends on the type's constraints.
Source: [PostgreSQL Numeric Types](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/datatype-numeric.html).

**Implication:** money/quantity need a consistent decimal in Go, database and
JSON. Validate finite values and domain limits in the application and in the schema;
using `NUMERIC` alone does not validate financial data. Define rounding
per instrument and operation. BRL and USD must not be added without FX and instant/source.

**Verified:** row locks and advisory locks exist, with transaction/session semantics
and the possibility of deadlock.
Source: [PostgreSQL Explicit Locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/explicit-locking.html).

**Implication:** serialize risk reservation per account and record intent/outbox
in the same transaction. If using a session advisory lock, keep affinity with the
dedicated connection; a pool may invalidate assumptions. Plan for timeout, lock ordering
and recovery. A database lock alone does not prevent an external request already in flight:
idempotency and reconciliation remain necessary.

**Pending:** TimescaleDB availability was not proven on the Railway service that
will be created. Conventional PostgreSQL remains the initial proposal; extension,
Redis and NATS require their own justification and verification before becoming a dependency.

## 8. Railway: continuous execution and deploy

### 8.1 Migrations

**Verified:** the pre-deploy runs in a separate container, without mounted volumes;
filesystem changes do not persist. The configuration allows limiting its duration.
Source: [Railway Pre-deploy](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/pre-deploy-command).

**Implication:** run migrations against the database over the network, with timeout and
serialization. Do not depend on SQLite/local file in this step. Apply changes
compatible with old code during version overlap.

### 8.2 Start command

**Verified:** for a Docker image, the start command overrides ENTRYPOINT in
exec form; shell variables require an explicit shell.
Source: [Railway Start Command](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/start-command).

**Implication:** provide complete binaries. The starter only has `/app/app`; the
planned `/app/engine` must be implemented and included in the Dockerfile.
Do not configure a service to run a binary that does not yet exist.

### 8.3 Sleep, availability and monitoring

**Verified:** Serverless can put an inactive service to sleep and introduces cold start. The Railway healthcheck gates deploy activation, but does not perform
continuous monitoring after publication.
Sources: [Serverless](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/serverless)
and [Healthchecks](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/healthchecks).

**Implication:** disable sleeping on the engine and create external heartbeat/alerts.
Do not rely on incidental traffic or an open tab. Separate liveness, readiness and the
account's operational state. A live process may be blocked by risk.

### 8.4 Replicas

**Verified:** traffic is distributed among replicas and there are no sticky
sessions according to the consulted documentation.
Source: [Railway Scaling](https://docs.railway.com/deployments/scaling).

**Implication:** share OAuth state/sessions when needed. A configured replica does not
replace leadership/fencing in the worker during deploy. Test
overlap, SIGTERM and recovery before allowing automatic paper orders.

## 9. Quantitative research and backtest limits

**Verified:** the QuantConnect guide describes overfitting, look-ahead and survivorship bias; it recommends walk-forward evaluation and distinguishes information
published later from data available at the simulated instant.
Source: [QuantConnect Research Guide](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/key-concepts/research-guide).

**Implication:** store strategy version, data, adjustments, costs and seed; reserve an
untouched temporal test; record attempts and discarded results;
use only information available at that instant. Do not select only current winning stocks
to claim generalizable results. Separate in-sample, out-of-sample and paper performance.

Proposed implementation rules: closed candles generate eligible orders on the
next event; OHLC does not determine the intrabar order between stop and target. Use an
explicit conservative assumption or finer data, costs and sensitivity to liquidity.
These rules are simulator choices, not properties guaranteed by the data.

## 10. Classification and financial promises

**Verified:** the CVM distinguishes investment robots for advisory, management and orders. The page
covers service provision requirements and warns against promised profitability. It also distinguishes systems that operationalize the investor's own decisions from certain offers of standardized strategies.
Source: [CVM — investment robots](https://www.gov.br/investidor/pt-br/investir/como-investir/profissionais-do-mercado/robos-de-investimentos).

**Implication:** keep the confirmed personal scope and transparent metrics in the MVP,
without any promise of gain. Turning the terminal into SaaS/signals/management requires another
analysis of the service actually offered. This research does not establish exemption, mandatory registration specific to vukem-trading, tax calculation or legal opinion.

## Addendum — Railway validated during publication (2026-11-03)

Primary source consulted: [https://docs.railway.com/builds/dockerfiles#cache-mounts](https://docs.railway.com/builds/dockerfiles#cache-mounts)
(expansion of [https://docs.railway.com/guides/dockerfiles](https://docs.railway.com/guides/dockerfiles)).
The page requires literal cache IDs `s/<service id>-<target path>` and states
that variables cannot be used in these IDs. The real builder rejected mounts
without an ID and secret-type mounts. The Dockerfile was simplified to conventional
layers, shared between web and engine, without binding to IDs.

The authenticated API returned an error when setting `railwayConfigFile`: legacy Config as Code
was discontinued and the vendor recommends IaC. The current GraphQL schema does not
include `DOCKERFILE` in `Builder`, but `dockerfilePath` makes detection work.
The configuration was validated via `serviceInstanceUpdate`, without these two fields,
with migrations pre-deploy, readiness and hibernation disabled. See ADR-0014.
The web was published and `/login` returned HTTP 200 on the Railway domain. This does not
validate Google OAuth nor the broker, whose credentials are still placeholders/empty.

## Pesquisa 2026-11-14

Escopo: pesquisa para o recomendador (ações B3 e EUA, cripto) e para o bot de day trade em paper trading e backtest. Nada aqui ativa integração, ordem real ou decisão aceita. Fontes oficiais foram abertas quando possível; quando a página não abriu ou não trouxe o dado, está marcado como não verificado. Preços e limites mudam: revalidar antes de contratar.

### Resumo executivo

- **A meta de 10 a 20% ao dia não é realista.** Composto de 10% por dia durante 250 pregões dá fator de aproximadamente 2,2 x 10^10; 20% por dia dá 6,2 x 10^19 (conta própria, sem custos). Nenhuma fonte encontrada sustenta essa meta; a melhor evidência disponível aponta o contrário (seção 4).
- **Day trade de varejo perde dinheiro na média.** No estudo brasileiro de Chague, De-Losso e Giovannetti, 97% dos que persistiram mais de 300 pregões perderam dinheiro; só 0,4% ganhou mais que um caixa de banco por dia.
- **Dados gratuitos existem, mas com limites duros e atraso.** Para o recomendador diário (ações e cripto, janela de 30 dias), um plano gratuito ou barato basta se atraso de 15 a 60 minutos for aceitável. Nenhuma fonte gratuita verificada entrega B3 intraday.
- **Paper trading automatizado é viável nos EUA** (Alpaca Paper, plano Basic) e em cripto (Binance testnet). Para B3 não encontrei documentação oficial de API de corretora; a rota existente (replay interno) continua a mais segura.
- **Regulação:** a CVM não dá regra direta para robô que recomenda ações sem supervisão humana. Um bot que ranqueia ativos com estratégia padronizada pode cair na exigência de analista credenciado. Disclaimer e parecer jurídico são necessários antes de divulgar ou cobrar.
- **Telegram:** em chat individual, evitar mais de 1 mensagem por segundo; em grupo, no máximo 20 por minuto; acima disso, erro 429.

### 2. Execução automatizada e paper trading

### 3. Regulação e impostos (Brasil)

**CVM sobre robôs.** A página oficial da CVM separa robôs consultores (recomendam e o investidor executa), robôs gestores (investem por conta do cliente) e robôs de ordens (automatizam entradas, saídas e envio de ordens). Sobre credenciamento, a página cita o Ofício-Circular 2/2019: a necessidade de credenciamento é restrita aos serviços que envolvem estratégias pré-definidas, e não abrange quem comercializa apenas sistemas automatizados. Fonte: [https://www.gov.br/investidor/pt-br/investir/como-investir/profissionais-do-mercado/robos-de-investimentos](https://www.gov.br/investidor/pt-br/investir/como-investir/profissionais-do-mercado/robos-de-investimentos).

- **Implicação para o recomendador:** se o bot ranqueia ativos e envia recomendação com estratégia padronizada, há risco de ser lido como serviço de análise de valores mobiliários (Resolução CVM 20/2021, credenciamento de analista). A CVM não traz regra direta para robô que gera recomendações sem supervisão humana; não encontrei a resposta oficial nesta rodada. A Resolução CVM 20 consolidada está em [https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/001/resol020consolid.pdf](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/export/sites/cvm/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/001/resol020consolid.pdf), não lida integralmente.
- **Disclaimer recomendado, independente da resposta final:** conteúdo informativo, sem oferta nem recomendação individual de investimento; rentabilidade passada não garante resultado; o bot não envia ordens; o usuário decide e responde pelas próprias operações. Consultar advogado antes de cobrar, divulgar publicamente ou vender assinatura.
- **Sinal da CVM sobre day trade:** a mesma página diz que não há estudos indicando que robôs aumentem a lucratividade de pessoas físicas.

**Imposto de renda.**

- Day trade de ações: alíquota de 20% sobre o ganho líquido mensal (fontes secundárias de corretoras e contadorias; a página da Receita sobre DARF aberta não traz os códigos). Código 6015 é citado por várias fontes para renda variável.
- IRRF de 1% sobre o ganho de day trade e 0,005% em swing, com dedução do DARF mensal. A base da retenção (bruto ou líquido diário) diverge entre fontes. Base normativa: IN RFB 1.585/2015, [https://www.normaslegais.com.br/legislacao/instrucao-normativa-1585-2015.htm](https://www.normaslegais.com.br/legislacao/instrucao-normativa-1585-2015.htm) (texto não aberto nesta rodada; a regra veio de fontes secundárias).
- Cripto: a isenção para vendas mensais até R$ 35 mil e sua aplicação ao day trade de cripto divergem entre as fontes. Código de DARF de cripto citado como 4600 em várias fontes, não confirmado na Receita. Declaração de Criptoativos (IN RFB 2.291/2025) citada como vigente na prática desde 2026-07-01, por fontes secundárias.
- **Não verificado oficialmente:** alíquotas de cripto, código de DARF de cripto e base do IRRF. Não usar esses valores no código sem confirmação na Receita ou com contador. Referência geral: [https://www.gov.br/receitafederal](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal).

### 4. Evidência sobre day trade de varejo

**Brasil (Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti, "Day Trading for a Living?").** Amostra de traders de mini-índice futuro na B3 que começaram entre 2013 e 2015. Resultados no resumo: 97% dos que persistiram mais de 300 pregões perderam dinheiro; 0,4% ganhou mais que um caixa de banco (cerca de US$ 54/dia); o melhor individual teve média diária de US$ 310. Os autores apontam limite de representatividade e não consideram imposto de renda nem custos de curso e plataforma. Fonte: [https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:spa:wpaper:2019wpecon47](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:spa:wpaper:2019wpecon47). Fontes secundárias citam 1,1% acima do salário mínimo e 7,9% que persistiu mais de 300 pregões (de 19.646 iniciantes); as contagens divergem entre versões (1.551 em uma, 554 em outra descrita pela imprensa). Usar o resumo oficial, não os números secundários. Imprensa: [https://www.bloombergquint.com/onweb/day-traders-in-brazil-study-would-ve-done-better-with-9-to-5-job](https://www.bloombergquint.com/onweb/day-traders-in-brazil-study-would-ve-done-better-with-9-to-5-job).

**Taiwan (Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean, "The cross-section of speculator skill", JFM 2014).** Traders de 1992 a 2006. Os 500 melhores no ano anterior ganharam em média 61,3 bps/dia antes de taxas e 37,9 bps/dia depois; os piores perderam -28,9 bps/dia depois de taxas. Menos de 1% da população de day traders ganha de forma previsível e confiável, líquido de taxas. Fonte: [https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:finmar:v:18:y:2014:i:c:p:1-24](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:finmar:v:18:y:2014:i:c:p:1-24). O estudo anterior (2004, "Do Individual Day Traders Make Money?") é citado em fontes secundárias com mais de 80% perdendo após custos; não abri o texto original.

**Mensagem para o produto:** a evidência não sustenta 10 a 20% ao dia nem qualquer promessa de lucro. O bot de day trade deve ser apresentado como ferramenta de teste (backtest e paper), com métricas de custo, drawdown e número de trades. Não encontrei retornos algorítmicos publicados e verificados em nível de "retorno líquido por dia" para varejo; não há número a citar.

### 5. Screening e backtest honesto

**Métodos para o recomendador (ações e cripto, 30 dias).** Não consegui abrir um estudo primário com números pós-custo para momentum de 30 dias em B3 ou cripto. Indícios de fontes secundárias: momentum cross-sectional de 12 meses com exclusão do último mês (12-1) é o padrão da literatura (Jegadeesh e Titman, 1993); o horizonte de 1 mês tende a reverter; momentum de série temporal tem evidência forte em futuros, mas a replicação em ações é contestada. Não verificado: retorno líquido desses sinais no mercado brasileiro. Sinais a considerar, sem promessa de retorno: retorno de 30 dias normalizado por volatilidade, força relativa contra índice (IBOV, S&P 500), volume relativo à mediana de 60 dias, z-score de desvio da média para reversão e filtro de liquidez (volume financeiro mínimo).

**Backtest honesto (checklist com fonte).**

- Look-ahead: sinais só com dados já fechados e teste de perturbação do futuro (ADR-0016 e `TestNoStrategyLooksAhead`, já no projeto). Ver também `docs/trading/freqtrade.md`.
- Survivorship bias: o universo precisa incluir papéis deslistados ou documentar que não inclui (`docs/trading/backtesting.md`).
- Overfitting e múltiplos testes: Probability of Backtest Overfitting (Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado, Zhu) [https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253); Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey e López de Prado, 2014) [https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551). O projeto já tem DSR (fase 9.6).
- Custos e slippage: o projeto já modela custo e slippage; falta documentar a calibração com dados reais.

**Frameworks em Go (nenhum dominante verificado):**

- `gobacktest`, event-driven, poucas estrelas, descrito como em desenvolvimento: [https://github.com/gobacktest/gobacktest](https://github.com/gobacktest/gobacktest).
- `xoney`, sem dependências, event-driven, ordens a mercado e limite: [https://github.com/quick-trade/xoney](https://github.com/quick-trade/xoney).
- `wisp`, backtest e trading em Go com TUI, multi-exchange: [https://github.com/wisp-trading/wisp](https://github.com/wisp-trading/wisp).
- Não encontrei framework Go vetorizado maduro. Maturidade de todos os itens não verificada (não abri histórico de commits). Recomendação: manter o motor próprio do projeto, que já é determinístico e testado; usar os frameworks só como referência.

### 6. Telegram Bot API (alertas)

Fonte oficial: [https://core.telegram.org/bots/faq](https://core.telegram.org/bots/faq).

- Em um chat: evitar mais de 1 mensagem por segundo. Rajadas curtas podem passar, mas depois vêm erros 429.
- Em grupo: no máximo 20 mensagens por minuto.
- Broadcast gratuito: cerca de 30 mensagens por segundo; acima disso, 429. Broadcast pago (Stars, ativado em @BotFather) sobe o teto a 1.000/s, com custo de 0,1 Star por mensagem acima de 30/s.
- Boas práticas: fila com limite por chat, retry com backoff em 429 respeitando o `retry_after`, deduplicação (o projeto já dispara cada alerta uma vez) e agrupamento de notificações em uma mensagem.
